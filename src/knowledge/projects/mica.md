# MICA

**Problem & Context:** How can technology help younger and older people find better housing arrangements in Milan? MICA tackles the intersecting crises of housing shortages and urban loneliness through intergenerational co-living.
**Role:** Lead UX Designer.
**Research & Insights:** Explored emotionally intelligent housing experiences, focusing on the friction points of matching different generations under one roof.
**Design Decisions:** Designed a service and digital product that fosters belonging.
**My Contribution:** Worked across research, service design, UX, and interaction design.
