Heritage Brand Food Discovery Concept Design

What is ORCO?

ORCO is a heritage Italian sauce and condiment brand built on generations of craftsmanship and ingredient quality. This project explored how its digital experience could evolve from a traditional product-focused website into a more engaging culinary platform.

My Role

As UX/UI Designer, I led research and visual direction — shaping how ORCO's heritage could become something people actively explore

CONTEXT

What if a sauce brand could be explored, not just bought?

ORCO's strength is its history — but most visitors only ever see a product list. The opportunity was to turn that heritage into an experience.

Consumers increasingly want to understand what they are buying, where it comes from, how it is made, and how it fits into their lifestyle.

Tracing ingredients from sourcing to bottle became a central part of the concept.

RESEARCH

Discovery Phase

Competitive & Food-Commerce Benchmarking

I mapped competitor digital experiences and food-commerce patterns to find where ORCO could differentiate — and where heritage could become a genuine advantage.

Key Finding #1: Shoppers want to understand, not just purchase.

People increasingly ask where it comes from, how it's made, and how it fits their life.

Key Finding #2: Recipes are the natural entry point.

Users often arrive for "a 15-minute dinner" — not for a specific sauce.

Key Finding #3: Storytelling builds trust better than claims.

Making craftsmanship visible earned more belief than stating "quality ingredients."

SOLUTION

I designed ORCO's visual direction to feel heritage yet contemporary — balancing craft and warmth with a modern, appetizing digital experience.

Feature #1: Interactive Recipes

Discover recipes through tags such as Vegan, Keto, 15 min, High Protein, Spicy, and Easy.

Recipe discovery is organised around lifestyle and dietary needs, so users find a meal first and ORCO second.

Feature #2: Ingredient Journey

Follow ingredients from sourcing → production → bottle → table through an interactive timeline and map.

Transparency becomes an experience: users can trace exactly where each ingredient comes from and how it becomes the final product.

Feature #3: Authenticity & Process

Make ORCO's craftsmanship visible through the rich history of ORCO's journey showing its evolution through time

Instead of claiming quality, the experience shows it — turning heritage into something users can actually explore.

CONCEPT VALIDATION

Does it give a reason to explore?

The concepts were evaluated through competitive benchmarking, UX walkthroughs, information architecture testing, prototype exploration, and comparison against existing food-brand experiences.

The final concept demonstrated how ORCO could evolve from a product catalogue into a digital culinary ecosystem — connecting its products, ingredients, heritage, recipes and community in one experience.

From "What sauce do you sell?" → to "What can I discover, cook, and experience with ORCO?"

WHERE ORCO GOES FROM HERE

  • Personalize the experience with CRM and preference-based touches built from recipe and dietary signals
  • Extend the framework into seasonal campaigns tied to ingredients and occasions
  • Scale the platform across ORCO's full product range and future campaigns

KEY TAKEAWAYS

01 — Heritage can be a digital asset.

ORCO's history becomes more valuable when people can actually explore it.

02 — Transparency can become an experience.

Instead of simply saying "quality ingredients," show users where they come from and how they become the final product.

03 — Recipes can become the entry point.

People may not arrive looking for a sauce. They may arrive looking for "a 15-minute vegan dinner."

04 — Brand websites can be more than storefronts.

The opportunity is to create a platform where discovery, education, cooking and community come together.
