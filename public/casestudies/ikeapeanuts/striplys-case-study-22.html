Striplys
Window Talks

Synthesizing Scandinavian functionalism and Peanuts' emotional honesty into a spatial podcast ecosystem.

Brief Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual narrative platform that reimagines IKEA showrooms as immersive emotional archives.

The project transforms personal stories into visual narratives using the Striplys Window — a real-time memory visualizer recorded live within authentic living simulations.

Metadata Role: UX / Service Designer
Timeline: Conceptual Project
Impact: Narrative Service Design

Problem Statement

Modern retail environments often prioritize transactions over connection. While IKEA creates simulations of life, there is a missing emotional bridge between the product and the user's lived history.

Digital audiences are seeking "slower" content — authentic, reflective experiences that ground them in a world of high-velocity noise.

Challenge How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for connection while aligning with the nostalgic language of Peanuts?

Objectives

  • Create Emotional Brand Engagement: Connect through stories rather than ads.
  • Integrate Peanuts Meaningfully: Rooted in nostalgia and emotional honesty.
  • Redefine Podcast Formats: Transform audio into a spatial experience.
  • Build an Experiential Retail Ecosystem: Active emotional environments.
Core Thesis Brands that make space for vulnerability earn lasting connection.

Research & Insights

Research revealed that IKEA spaces are tied to life transitions. Peanuts provides the vocabulary for the vulnerability inherent in those moments.

Archetypes Charlie Brown — Vulnerability
Linus — Reflection
Snoopy — Imagination
"People do not emotionally remember products — they remember moments."

2. Platforms & Participant Recruitment

Recruitment is split into two primary channels to capture authentic memories from IKEA's global community.

Online Channels Direct CTA forms hosted on the official IKEA website and integrated social media platforms.
Offline Rituals Physical "Story Boxes" placed in-store, facilitating a lucky-draw style ritual for anonymous memory submission.
