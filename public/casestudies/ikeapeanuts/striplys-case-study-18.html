Striplys Window Talks

Designing an Emotional Podcast Experience Through an IKEA × Peanuts Collaboration.

Project Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual podcast and experiential platform that reimagines IKEA showrooms as immersive emotional archives. It explores how conversations about home can be visualized in real-time.

METADATA ROLE: UX / Service Designer
TIMELINE: Conceptual Project
IMPACT: Narrative Service Design

Problem Statement

Modern retail environments often prioritize transactions over connection. While IKEA creates physical simulations of life, there is a missing emotional bridge between the product and the user's lived history.

CHALLENGE How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for connection while aligning with the nostalgic language of Peanuts?

Objectives

  • Create Emotional Brand Engagement: Connect through stories rather than advertisements.
  • Integrate Peanuts Meaningfully: Rooted in nostalgia and emotional honesty.
  • Redefine Podcast Formats: Transform audio into a spatial, multi-sensory experience.

Research & Insights

Research revealed that IKEA spaces are tied to life transitions. Peanuts provides the vocabulary for the vulnerability inherent in those moments.

03 / System & Logic

Based on the architectural blueprint of the collaboration, the project operates as a high-fidelity multi-platform ecosystem. The system ensures consistent quality while scaling across diverse IKEA retail environments.

RECORD & RELEASE

"A Story Shared, With Care."

The transition from the physical showroom stage to the digital earbud is a delicate editorial journey. We capture live human vulnerability and translate it into a poetic narrative for a global audience.

1. Logistics & Schedule

The podcast operates on a rigid 25-minute conversational rhythm, optimized for retention and emotional pacing. Released weekly on Sundays at 2:00 PM, it becomes a ritual for the "slower" weekend listener.

  • MEDIUM: Audio + Video Hybrid (Full podcast + Social Clips)
  • RHYTHM: 12m Convo / 1m Snoopy Break / 12m Reflection
  • SPECIALS: Back to School (Aug), Spring Renewal (Mar), Mental Health Day (Oct)

2. Platforms & Participant Recruitment

Recruitment maps from OOH discovery to a physical, anonymous submission ritual. The strategy bridges the digital IKEA app with the physical showroom floor.

ONLINE RECRUITMENT

Call-to-action forms hosted on the Official IKEA Website and social platforms for memory sharing.

OFFLINE RECRUITMENT

Physical "Story Boxes" (resembling lucky draw boxes) placed in-store for visitors to drop memories.

PLATFORM_STRATEGY

Primary integration through the IKEA Official App and mainstream podcast channels (Apple/Spotify), ensuring a seamless jump from showroom to personal device.

3. Quality Assurance & Production Strategy

To ensure authentic vulnerability and narrative depth, we implement a five-step editorial framework for every guest and story selected.

  • CURATED PROFILES: Reviewing participant backgrounds.
  • TAILORED CULTURE: Adapting content for local resonance.
  • PRE-INTERVIEWS: Vetting and preparing guests.
  • SEMI-SCRIPTED FLOW: Balancing authenticity with structure.
  • EDITORIAL SELECTION: Finalizing the best stories for the cut.
