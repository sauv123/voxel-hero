A Narrative UX Case Study

Striplys Window Talks

Transforming IKEA showrooms into emotional archives through Peanuts-inspired storytelling.
Emotional Security

01 / The Problem

Modern retail environments often prioritize transactions over connection. While IKEA creates physical simulations of life, there is a missing emotional bridge between the product and the user's lived history.

Digital audiences are seeking "slower" content—authentic, reflective experiences that ground them in a world of high-velocity noise.

THE_CHALLENGE

How might we transform retail space into a platform for human connection while aligning with the nostalgic language of Peanuts?

02 / Insights & Values

Research revealed that IKEA spaces are intrinsically tied to life transitions—moving out, starting over, building nests. Peanuts provides the vocabulary for the vulnerability inherent in those moments.

Behavioral_Data

PEOPLE DO NOT REMEMBER THE CHAIR. THEY REMEMBER THE CONVERSATION THEY HAD SITTING IN IT.

01. SPATIAL SIMULATION: The Showroom as Stage
02. VALUE CLUSTERS: The Emotional Architecture of Peanuts

By mapping character archetypes to IKEA room sets, we developed three emotional pillars: Emotional Comfort (Linus), Nostalgic Minimalism (Peanuts style), and Imaginative Practicality (Snoopy).

ARCHETYPES.LOG

• Charlie Brown → Vulnerability
• Linus → Reflection
• Snoopy → Imagination

03 / Approach

The experience is a multi-sensory storytelling ecosystem. We treated furniture not as display stock, but as narrative participants. Each theme was strategically paired with a specific environment.

04 / The Experience Flow

01
ARRIVAL & LOWERING BARRIERS

Guests enter a curated room set. Lighting is warm, textiles are layered, and Peanuts-inspired "marginalia" (doodles) inhabit the space to lower emotional shields.

02
PEANUTS REFLECTION CARDS

A series of prompts inspired by the comics—"What object holds your favorite routine?"—create an immediate shift from product-talk to memory-talk.

03
MAIN CONVERSATION

Intimate storytelling facilitate by a Linus-inspired host style. The environment shift from a "showroom" to a "confession space."

"The home is an emotional archive."

The Striplys Window

The memory visualizer. A 4×4 LED grid that dynamically translates the guest's spoken history into symbolic comic-strip animations.

05 / Results

FOR_IKEA

Stronger emotional brand loyalty through narrative ownership and increased engagement duration.

FOR_PEANUTS

Renewed relevance for a younger generation by integrating nostalgia into modern lifestyle culture.

Final Reflection

Striplys Window Talks reinforced that design is about the spaces where people feel understood. It's a study on how narrative can shape the very environments we inhabit.
