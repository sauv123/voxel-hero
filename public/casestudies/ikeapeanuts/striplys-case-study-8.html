Project Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual podcast and experiential storytelling platform designed as a collaboration between IKEA and Peanuts. The project transforms IKEA showrooms into emotionally immersive spaces where conversations about home, identity, nostalgia, vulnerability, and comfort become shared experiences.

Rather than creating a traditional branded campaign, the project explores how storytelling, spatial design, and emotional interaction can transform retail environments into meaningful human experiences.

Problem Statement

Modern retail environments often prioritize products and transactions over emotional engagement. While IKEA successfully creates inspirational living environments, there is an opportunity to deepen the emotional relationship between users and the concept of “home.”

"How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for storytelling, comfort, and human connection while aligning with the nostalgic emotional language of Peanuts?"

Digital audiences increasingly seek slower, more authentic, and emotionally meaningful content experiences. Most podcasts today are visually repetitive and detached from physical environments.

Objectives

  • Create Emotional Brand Engagement: Design an experience that allows people to emotionally connect through stories rather than advertisements.
  • Integrate Peanuts Meaningfully: Use Peanuts as a storytelling framework rooted in nostalgia and emotional honesty.
  • Redefine the Podcast Format: Transform podcasting into a spatial, multi-sensory experience.
  • Build an Experiential Retail Ecosystem: Use IKEA spaces as active emotional environments instead of passive showroom displays.

Research & Insights

Brand Research — IKEA

Research revealed a recurring emotional philosophy: togetherness, everyday life improvement, and functional comfort. IKEA environments are designed to simulate real life and emotional familiarity.

PEOPLE DO NOT EMOTIONALLY REMEMBER PRODUCTS — THEY REMEMBER MOMENTS, FEELINGS, AND SPACES CONNECTED TO THEIR LIVES.

Key Behavioral Insight: People often associate IKEA spaces with life transitions—moving out, first apartments, or new beginnings. This emotional relationship became the project's foundation.

Brand Research — Peanuts

Peanuts revealed emotional themes deeply compatible with IKEA: vulnerability, imperfection, and quiet humor. Characters function as archetypes: Charlie Brown (Vulnerability), Linus (Reflection), Snoopy (Imagination).

Design Direction

01. Emotional Comfort

Lighting, seating, and sound designed to make vulnerability feel safe.

02. Nostalgic Minimalism

Combining Peanuts’ simplicity with IKEA’s Scandinavian minimalism.

03. Imaginative Practicality

Treating IKEA products as emotional objects (e.g., a lamp as a memory of late-night study).

The Solution

Striplys Window Talks is a multi-sensory storytelling ecosystem recorded inside real IKEA spaces. Personal stories are transformed into visual narratives via the Striplys Window.

The Podcast Experience: Episode Flow

PHASE 01: ARRIVAL INTO THE SPACE

Guests enter a carefully designed IKEA room environment treated as a lived-in emotional space: warm lighting, soft textiles, and minimal visual noise.

PHASE 02: PEANUTS QUESTION CARDS

Reflective prompts like "What space made you feel safest growing up?" or "What object in your home carries the most memory?" lower emotional barriers.

PHASE 03: MAIN CONVERSATION

The host (inspired by Linus) facilitates a quiet living-room conversation rather than an aggressive interview.

The Striplys Window

The name combines "Strip" (Peanuts comic strips) and "Lys" (Swedish for light). It is a modular 4×4 LED installation that acts as a real-time memory visualizer.

Spatial Design Strategy

IKEA showrooms become active emotional interfaces. Each podcast theme is matched to a specific room environment:

Methodology

Results & Impact

FOR IKEA

Stronger emotional brand loyalty, increased experiential engagement, and differentiation through emotional retail.

FOR PEANUTS

Renewed cultural relevance and expansion into modern experiential storytelling for younger audiences.

UX IMPACT

Expanded UX thinking into environmental storytelling, spatial interaction, and narrative systems beyond screens.

Final Reflection

This project reinforced that design is not only about usability. It is about memory, comfort, and the spaces where people feel understood. Striplys Window Talks became an exploration of how design can make people feel emotionally safe, seen, and connected through the language of home.
