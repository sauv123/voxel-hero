Striplys Window Talks

Designing an Emotional Podcast Experience Through an IKEA × Peanuts Collaboration.

Project Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual podcast and experiential storytelling platform that reimagines IKEA showrooms as immersive emotional environments. It explores how conversations about home and identity can be transformed into spatial experiences.

METADATA ROLE: UX / Service Designer
TIMELINE: Conceptual Project
IMPACT: Narrative Service Design

Problem Statement

Modern retail environments often prioritize transactions over connection. While IKEA creates physical simulations of life, there is a missing emotional bridge between the product and the user's lived history.

Digital audiences are seeking "slower" content—authentic, reflective experiences that ground them in a world of high-velocity noise.

CHALLENGE How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for connection while aligning with the nostalgic language of Peanuts?

Objectives

  • Create Emotional Brand Engagement: Connect through stories rather than advertisements.
  • Integrate Peanuts Meaningfully: Rooted in nostalgia and emotional honesty.
  • Redefine Podcast Formats: Transform audio into a multi-sensory experience.
  • Build an Experiential Retail Ecosystem: Active emotional environments.

Research & Insights

Research revealed that IKEA spaces are intrinsically tied to life transitions—moving out, starting over, building nests. Peanuts provides the vocabulary for the vulnerability inherent in those moments.

Approach & Methodology

The experience is a multi-sensory storytelling ecosystem. We treated furniture not as display stock, but as narrative participants.

Each podcast theme was strategically paired with a specific IKEA room environment to ground the emotional tone in physical space.

ThemeEnvironment
VulnerabilityBedroom Setup
TogethernessLiving Room
NostalgiaChildren's Section
IndependenceSmall Apartment

Implementation / Flow

01
ARRIVAL & ATMOSPHERE

The guest enters a curated IKEA set. Lighting and textiles are layered to lower emotional barriers, creating a "safe house" for storytelling.

02
QUESTION CARDS

Reflective prompts like "What space made you feel safest growing up?" facilitate immediate emotional accessibility.

03
MAIN CONVERSATION

A quiet living-room conversation unfolding naturally, facilitated by an empathetic, Linus-inspired host style.

The Striplys Window

The technological heart. As guests share stories, the 4×4 grid translates their words into symbolic, comic-strip animations in real-time.

Results & Impact

FOR IKEA

Increased brand loyalty through emotional engagement and longer in-store interaction times.

FOR PEANUTS

Renewed cultural relevance for younger generations through modern, spatial storytelling.

Final Reflection

This project reinforced that design is about the spaces where people feel understood. Striplys Window Talks is a study on how narrative can shape the environments we inhabit.
