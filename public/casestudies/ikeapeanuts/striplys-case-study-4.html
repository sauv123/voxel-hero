Striplys Window Talks

Designing an Emotional Podcast Experience Through an IKEA × Peanuts Collaboration.

SS
By Case Study Author · May 14, 2026

The Striplys Window: A modular LED storytelling interface visualizing spoken memories through comic-strip animations.

Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual experiential podcast designed as a collaboration between IKEA and Peanuts. The project reimagines IKEA stores as emotional storytelling environments where conversations about memory, comfort, identity, and home become immersive experiences.

Problem Statement

Modern retail experiences often prioritize product visibility over emotional connection. While IKEA creates inspiring physical environments, there is an opportunity to deepen the relationship between people and the idea of “home.”

"How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for storytelling, comfort, and human connection?"

Research & Insights

Research into IKEA revealed themes of emotional comfort, simplicity, and togetherness. Simultaneously, Peanuts insights highlighted vulnerability, childhood nostalgia, and emotional honesty.

Core Insight

People do not emotionally remember products — they remember moments, feelings, and spaces connected to their lives.

Brand Research: Mapping overlapping emotional territories.
Spatial Mapping: Environment as a storytelling stage.
Journey Mapping: Emotional transitions in space.
Interaction: The Striplys Window interface.

Implementation

The Solution

A multi-sensory storytelling ecosystem recorded inside real IKEA spaces, where personal stories are transformed into visual narratives via the Striplys Window.

Instead of creating a traditional branded campaign, the goal was to build an experience where people could emotionally connect with the idea of home through conversation, memory, and storytelling.

Core Concept: Homes as Archives

At the center of the experience is the idea that homes are emotional archives. Furniture, objects, and lighting quietly hold routines, fears, and identity. The solution explores these connections through three layers:

  • Real human storytelling (Authenticity)
  • Peanuts-inspired emotional warmth (Nostalgia)
  • IKEA’s philosophy of everyday living (Practicality)

The Podcast Experience

Each episode features a real guest and a host inspired by Peanuts characters (like Linus). The episodes are intentionally calm, reflective, and visually warm—avoiding celebrity culture in favor of everyday human struggles and the meaning of home.

Episode Flow

01. WELCOME INTO THE SPACE

The guest enters a carefully designed IKEA room set treated like a lived-in emotional space: warm lighting, soft textiles, and minimal visual noise to lower emotional barriers.

02. PEANUTS QUESTION CARDS

Reflective prompts like "What space made you feel safest growing up?" or "What object in your home carries the most memory?" create emotional accessibility.

03. MAIN CONVERSATION

A quiet living-room conversation unfolding naturally. The host style is empathetic and observant, moving through gentle emotional exploration rather than aggressive interviewing.

The Striplys Window

The emotional centerpiece—a modular 4×4 LED storytelling installation. The name combines "Strip" (Peanuts) and "Lys" (Swedish for light).

As guests share stories, the Window responds dynamically: if a guest speaks of childhood storms, the window may animate rain outside a bedroom or the warm glow of a blanket fort in a poetic, comic-strip style.

Why It Matters

The Striplys Window transforms passive listening into visual storytelling. It creates a bridge between memory, imagination, and physical space, allowing audiences to experience the story rather than just hear it.

Results & Impact

For IKEA

Increased emotional brand loyalty, longer in-store engagement times, and enhanced experiential marketing opportunities.

For Peanuts

Renewed relevance for younger audiences and an expanded storytelling format integrated into modern lifestyle culture.
