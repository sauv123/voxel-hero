Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual experiential podcast designed as a collaboration between IKEA and Peanuts. The project reimagines IKEA stores as emotional storytelling environments where conversations about memory, comfort, identity, and home become immersive experiences.

Problem Statement

Modern retail experiences often prioritize product visibility over emotional connection. While IKEA creates inspiring physical environments, there is an opportunity to deepen the relationship between people and the idea of “home.”

"How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for storytelling, comfort, and human connection?"

Research & Insights

Research into IKEA revealed themes of emotional comfort, simplicity, and togetherness. Simultaneously, Peanuts insights highlighted vulnerability, childhood nostalgia, and emotional honesty.

STRIPLYS_CORE_PRINCIPLE

People do not emotionally remember products — they remember moments, feelings, and spaces connected to their lives.

Implementation

The Solution

Striplys Window Talks — An Emotional Podcast Experience Inside IKEA

The solution was designed as a multi-sensory storytelling ecosystem that combines podcasting, spatial design, emotional interaction, and brand experience into one cohesive service.

Instead of creating a traditional branded campaign, the goal was to build an experience where people could emotionally connect with the idea of home through conversation, memory, and storytelling.

Core Concept: Homes as Emotional Archives

Furniture, objects, lighting, and spaces quietly hold memories, routines, and identity. The solution explores these connections through intimate conversations staged inside IKEA environments.

  • Real human storytelling (Authenticity)
  • Peanuts-inspired emotional warmth (Nostalgia)
  • IKEA’s philosophy of everyday living (Practicality)

The Podcast Experience

Each episode features a real guest, a Peanuts-inspired host, and an emotionally themed conversation. The episodes are intentionally calm, reflective, and visually warm—creating an atmosphere that feels safe, nostalgic, and relatable.

Episode Flow

STEP 01: WELCOME INTO THE SPACE

The guest enters a carefully designed IKEA room set treated as a lived-in emotional space: warm lighting, soft textiles, and minimal visual noise to lower emotional barriers.

STEP 02: PEANUTS QUESTION CARDS

Reflective prompts like "What space made you feel safest growing up?" or "What object in your home carries the most memory?" create emotional accessibility and authentic storytelling.

STEP 03: MAIN CONVERSATION

The host—designed as calm, empathetic, and subtly inspired by Peanuts characters such as Linus—facilitates a quiet living-room conversation rather than an aggressive interview.

The Striplys Window

The emotional centerpiece—a modular 4×4 LED storytelling installation. The name combines "Strip" (Peanuts) and "Lys" (Swedish for light).

As guests share stories, the Window responds dynamically. If a guest speaks about childhood storms and building pillow forts, the window animates rain, blanket forts, and Snoopy-style illustrations in a poetic, comic-strip style.

Why It Matters

The Striplys Window transforms passive listening into visual storytelling. It creates a bridge between memory, imagination, and physical space, allowing audiences to experience the story visually and emotionally.

Results & Impact

For IKEA

Increased emotional brand loyalty, longer in-store engagement times, and enhanced experiential marketing opportunities.

For Peanuts

Renewed relevance for younger audiences and an expanded storytelling format integrated into modern lifestyle culture.

UX Impact

The project expanded traditional UX thinking beyond screens into environmental storytelling, emotional interaction, sensory design, and narrative-based engagement systems.

Key Takeaways

"Designing for emotion is not decorative — it is functional and foundational to memorable user experiences."

The project reinforced that storytelling is a UX tool, physical spaces can become interactive interfaces, and multi-sensory experiences increase meaning. Brand collaborations work best through shared emotional values.

Final Reflection

Striplys Window Talks became more than a podcast concept — it evolved into an exploration of how design can create comfort, nostalgia, and emotional connection in everyday spaces. It reinforced the belief that meaningful design is about how people feel, remember, and connect.
