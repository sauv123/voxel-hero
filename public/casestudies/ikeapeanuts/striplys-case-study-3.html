Striplys Window Talks

Designing an Emotional Podcast Experience Through an IKEA × Peanuts Collaboration.

SS
By Case Study Author · May 14, 2026

The Striplys Window: A modular LED storytelling interface visualizing spoken memories through comic-strip animations.

Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual experiential podcast designed as a collaboration between IKEA and Peanuts. The project reimagines IKEA stores as emotional storytelling environments where conversations about memory, comfort, identity, and home become immersive experiences.

The concept combines a live podcast format, Peanuts-inspired storytelling, spatial design inside IKEA environments, and an interactive installation called the Striplys Window — a modular LED storytelling wall that visualizes memories through comic-strip-inspired animations.

Problem Statement

Modern retail experiences often prioritize product visibility over emotional connection. While IKEA creates inspiring physical environments, there is an opportunity to deepen the relationship between people and the idea of “home.”

"How might IKEA transform retail space into a platform for storytelling, comfort, and human connection?"

Research & Insights

Research into IKEA revealed themes of emotional comfort, simplicity, and togetherness. Simultaneously, Peanuts insights highlighted vulnerability, childhood nostalgia, and emotional honesty.

Core Insight

People do not emotionally remember products — they remember moments, feelings, and spaces connected to their lives.

Brand Research: Mapping overlapping emotional territories.
Spatial Mapping: Environment as a storytelling stage.
Journey Mapping: Emotional transitions in space.
Interaction: The Striplys Window interface.

Audiences are increasingly seeking slower, more reflective digital experiences. Nostalgia creates stronger emotional retention and community interaction, making the IKEA × Peanuts pairing a powerful tool for engagement.

Approach / Methodology

The experience was designed using three emotional pillars: Emotional Comfort, Nostalgic Minimalism, and Imaginative Practicality.

Emotional Comfort

Creating spaces where vulnerability feels safe, using real IKEA room sets as storytelling stages.

Nostalgic Minimalism

Using simple visual language and childhood-inspired storytelling rooted in the Peanuts universe.

The podcast ecosystem extends beyond audio into environment, interaction, and memory. Real IKEA room sets—bedrooms for vulnerability, living rooms for connection—become intentional stages for the narrative.

Implementation

Each episode follows a consistent emotional rhythm: Introduction, Peanuts-inspired reflective questions, the main emotional conversation, the "Daydream Window" visual moment, and a home-related challenge.

The Striplys Window

A core experiential element—conceptualized as a 4×4 LED panel. The window dynamically visualizes stories and emotions shared during the podcast using animated comic-style scenes.

Design elements include a soft Scandinavian palette, warm ambient lighting, and comic-strip framing. Interactive elements like Peanuts Question Cards and plush characters further ground the experience in physical space.

Results & Impact

The final concept demonstrated that retail spaces can become emotional storytelling platforms and that brands can create meaningful engagement without traditional advertising.

  • For IKEA: Increased emotional brand loyalty and longer in-store engagement.
  • For Peanuts: Renewed relevance for younger audiences and expanded storytelling formats.
  • UX Impact: Expanded traditional thinking into environmental storytelling and sensory design.
