SCOPE IKEA x Peanuts
STATUS Conceptual

01 / Overview

Striplys Window Talks is a conceptual narrative platform that reimagines IKEA showrooms as immersive emotional archives. In collaboration with Peanuts, the project explores how conversations about memory and home can be visualized in real-time.

At the center sits the Striplys Window—a 4×4 LED interface that translates spoken memories into poetic, comic-strip animations.

02 / Insights

Research revealed a powerful synergy: IKEA creates the physical scaffolding of life, while Peanuts provides the emotional language for its vulnerability.

PEANUTS_ARCHETYPES Charlie Brown (Vulnerability) · Linus (Reflection) · Snoopy (Imagination)

INSIGHT_MANIFESTO.LOG

PEOPLE DO NOT REMEMBER PRODUCTS. THEY REMEMBER THE MOMENTS CONNECTED TO THEM.

Most podcasts today are detached from space. By staging conversations in bedrooms (Vulnerability) or living rooms (Connection), the furniture becomes a narrative participant rather than an object.

03 / Implementation

"Designing for emotion is not decorative—it is foundational to usability."
01
ARRIVAL & ATMOSPHERE

Guests enter a curated IKEA set. Lighting and textiles are layered to lower emotional barriers, creating a "safe house" for storytelling.

02
PEANUTS QUESTION CARDS

Conversation starters inspired by Peanuts themes: "What space made you feel safest?" or "What object holds your favorite routine?"

03
THE MAIN CONVERSATION

Intimate, slow-paced dialogue facilitated by an empathetic host, unfolding in a lived-in room set.

The Striplys Window

The memory visualizer. As guests share stories, the 4×4 grid translates their words into symbolic, comic-strip animations.

05 / Outcomes

FOR IKEA

Increased brand loyalty through emotional engagement and longer in-store interaction times.

FOR PEANUTS

Renewed cultural relevance for younger generations through modern, spatial storytelling.

06 / Reflection

This project reinforced that design is about the spaces where people feel understood. Striplys Window Talks is not just a podcast; it's a study on how narrative can shape the environments we inhabit.
