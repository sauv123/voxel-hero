KRIZIA

Architectural Luxury House

Art Direction Sauveer Sinha (Academic & Client Project) Time: 4 Months
Introduction

Beyond Excess

Luxury fashion has become saturated with excess. Branding, storytelling, speed. Heritage houses struggle to stay relevant while preserving depth of identity.

This project rebuilds Krizia as a contemporary luxury brand rooted in architectural precision, material honesty, and enduring design. Clothing is not seasonal decoration. It is engineered structure.

THE QUESTION

What if luxury was defined not by excess, but by structural integrity?

The Problem

Rediscovering Philosophy.

Krizia had an influential history in Italian fashion but no clear contemporary positioning. Its archive of experimental textiles and architectural silhouettes was not translating into a cohesive modern identity.

The challenge: rediscover a philosophy that justifies luxury through meaning, not spectacle.

The Strategic Reframe

Wearable Architecture.

Krizia went from heritage fashion house to architectural luxury system. Garments became engineered forms and material structures, not decoration.

POSITIONING

Designed for Endurance.
Engineered for Form.

Historical Research

Milanese Industrial Rigor.

Krizia's archives show a consistent relationship between structure and material experimentation. Founded by Mariuccia Mandelli, the house challenged Italian prêt-à-porter with metallic fabrics, sculptural tailoring, and broad-shouldered forms.

Mandelli's work aligned with Milanese modernism: architecture, industry, and intellectual rigor. Krizia was never decorative. It was structural.

Strategic Concept 01

The Structural Index™

Transparency as luxury. An NFC-enabled garment passport revealing pleating density, material composition, and origin verification.

Strategic Concept 02

The Drafting Table

Retail as an architect’s studio. A hybrid environment between material laboratory and gallery space for technical studies.

Strategic Concept 03

The Alchemic Material Library

Material as experience. An interactive installation showcasing cork polymer composites, metallic fabrics, and bio-engineered materials.

Website Prototype
Editorial Artifact

The Krizia Index.

A magazine-editorial artifact documenting the philosophy of the brand. KRIZIA INDEX merges material studies with cultural storytelling to build meaning beyond the product.

Results & Impact

Strategic Outcomes.

The project established a fully articulated luxury positioning system that:

  • differentiated Krizia from trend-based luxury competitors
  • connected historical heritage with future-facing innovation
  • created a scalable visual and experiential ecosystem

BRAND PERCEPTION SHIFT

The rebrand transformed Krizia from a heritage fashion label into a contemporary architectural luxury house centered on design intelligence and material innovation.

DESIGN IMPACT

The project demonstrated how transparency can become luxury, material storytelling can replace excessive branding, and fashion can function as engineered cultural design.
